# Validation decision after the passing ERPNext pair

Keep the next experiment about developer review and integration effort. The current evidence does not justify expanding automated repair orchestration or claiming superiority over competent CI. The plausible wedge remains making a narrow business rule, its application checks, exact change identity and assurance limits easy to review together. That usefulness is unmeasured.

## What is established

| Evidence | Supported conclusion | Limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Three DRF pairs](../../benchmarks/drf/README.md) | Both arms passed the selected tests/contracts; no observed feedback advantage | Researched historical cases, small sample, possible training contamination |
| [ERPNext lifecycle replay](../../benchmarks/erpnext/LIFECYCLE-RESULTS.md) | Both cached author candidates passed the same five application tests; complete controls/source identities/lifecycle under unchanged cap | One selected case and explicit prose requirement; no general repair effect |
| [Intermediate patch](../../benchmarks/erpnext/INTERMEDIATE-RESULTS.md) | Four earlier tests pass while a later read-denial obligation fails | Known followup, not independent discovery; adding the fifth test to ordinary CI also catches it |
| [Coverage review](../../benchmarks/erpnext/OBLIGATIONS.md) | Existing records can show named fixture scopes and exact verdicts, with unknown/incomplete evidence distinguished | Agent-authored interpretations of tests, not customer-approved policy or measured review savings |

The three DRF pairs and one ERPNext pair use different protocols; they are not a pooled causal benchmark. The [malformed raw-diff pair](../../benchmarks/erpnext/PAIRED-RESULTS.md), [failed structured setup](../../benchmarks/erpnext/STRUCTURED-RESULTS.md) and earlier provisioning failures remain separate and inconclusive. We did not re-author the valid ERPNext candidates to obtain a passing result.

The unchanged consistency kernel transferred across adapters, but that is evidence of prototype reuse, not a commercial advantage. The ERPNext application checks are ordinary tests, not a new formal proof. No human specification/integration/maintenance time, review-time saving, false-acceptance rate on an independently chosen population, or willingness to adopt/pay has been measured. The 620.399-second four-variant job is an observed experiment duration, not a production-CI overhead comparison.

## Fair alternatives

A competent CI comparison must use the same five checks, trusted expected behavior, complete failure/error/skip reporting and equally protected test/source configuration. It must not be weakened to a generic exit-code check or allowed to lose artifacts while ProofGate receives richer evidence. On the retained five-test observations, that test baseline has the same candidate pass/fail answers. We have not executed a separate comparative CI implementation or measured its engineering cost. Claiming incremental detection from the wrapper would be unsupported.

These documented capabilities already overlap with generic contracts, fixed criteria, feedback loops and receipts. This is not an exhaustive market survey or a claim about either tool's implementation quality. A narrow adapter or review artifact may be enough; building another general gate requires evidence beyond the current prototype.

## Next bounded decision

Use the [prepared review packet](DEVELOPER-REVIEW-PACKET.md) with two developers using coding agents on business applications, preferably unfamiliar with this historical case. Give the test-only and coverage presentations the same underlying source, requirement and test facts. Record comprehension, decision changes, time and maintenance objections; do not let an LLM stand in for participant responses.

Continue toward one real integration only if a participant supplies a concrete business rule/change and identifies a useful improvement over their existing workflow. If both presentations yield equally correct decisions at comparable effort, keep this as an experimental CI aid and do not infer a standalone product. New live execution would need a separately frozen scope/budget; no further source/model run is needed to share or review the packet.

The remaining input is an authorized reviewer/channel or an actual developer response, not another repair script. [The draft invitation](DEVELOPER-REVIEW-PACKET.md#unsent-invitation) is ready, but no outreach is authorized or sent. Without external feedback we can establish more technical properties, not whether this evidence changes a real developer's workflow. Production policy admission and deployment remain outside scope.
